Privacy Policy

WordPlay (iOS, Android and web) · Effective November 13, 2026

WordPlay is a word game. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for advertising. This page explains what the game stores, why, and how to delete it.

Data kept only on your device

To save your game, WordPlay stores the following on your phone or in your browser: the game in progress, your unlocks, stats, word-path history, theme and other settings, and your display name. It never leaves your device unless you use the online features below. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it.

Data stored online

Multiplayer, the daily challenge leaderboard and the "found worldwide" community counts need a server. The first time you use one of them, WordPlay creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no email address, password, phone number or real name. Linked to that ID we store:

What Why
Your display name Shown to the other players in your multiplayer games. It is random unless you change it; please don't use your real name.
Multiplayer games, moves and scores, and your place in the matchmaking queue To run games between players
Daily challenge results (date and number of moves) To stop duplicate entries and build the leaderboard
Which unlocks you have found, and the word paths you have played To count how many players worldwide have found each one

The leaderboard and community counts are published only as totals (for example, "1,204 players solved today's challenge in 6 moves"). They never show who you are.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the online database and the anonymous accounts.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Expo delivers app updates to the mobile app.

Like most online services, these providers may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, device type and request time) to run and secure their service. We don't use these logs to identify or track you.

How long we keep it

Your online data is kept until you delete it. Totals that no longer identify anyone (leaderboard counts, community counts) may remain after you delete your account.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: open the menu, then about, then delete my data. This permanently deletes your online account and everything linked to it, including multiplayer games you created, and clears everything stored on your device.
  • On the website: clear this site's data in your browser to remove what is stored locally. To delete an online account you used on the website, contact us at the address below.

Children

WordPlay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game never asks for an email, real name, location or contacts.
