# Reword — Launch Post Drafts

> **Purpose**: Ready-to-edit copy in the voice of [LAUNCH_PLAN.md](LAUNCH_PLAN.md) §3: bold, concise, curious, playful, mysterious. Discovery first, not anagram mechanics.

**Rules for every post (from the plan):**

- Never invent player stats, unlock rarity, word paths or testimonials. Any specific path in a post has to be one you actually played and verified in the game.
- Tease that mysteries exist; never post trigger words or the full unlock list.
- Don't state fixed counts of themes or bots ("75 themes"). Counts change, and the plan calls for accurate live numbers only.
- Read each community's self-promotion rules first. Never auto-post to Reddit or other communities.

Links: game `https://wordplay-blond.vercel.app` · Daily Challenge `https://wordplay-blond.vercel.app/?daily` (swap in the custom domain once it's chosen).

- **Headline:** Every word is a doorway.
